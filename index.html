Why it works

The brain checks three things before it lets you scroll past.

01

Reality violation

The brain detects "that shouldn't be possible" and stops to resolve the error - surreal physics, impossible objects, material swaps.

02

Signal salience

Something is so visually loud, novel, or charged it overrides the autopilot scroll - glitch, neon, extreme contrast.

03

Open loop

The brain is handed a puzzle it has to close - a door opening onto the unknown, a mask cracking, motion interrupted mid-frame.
